Large lots, longer sprinkler runs and outdoor water

LGI markets Orchard Park with large lots. A bigger yard often means more sprinkler heads, more hose bibs and more places for water to escape unseen.

Outdoor leaks hide. A wet strip of lawn, a sprinkler zone that never dries or a bill that jumps without a change in habits points to one. Five floor plans, up to three baths, one water heater

The plans run from three bedrooms and two baths to five bedrooms and three baths. The larger plans put more toilets, tubs and showers on the same hot water supply. If hot water fades during a busy morning, the heater's capacity, its thermostat setting and its heating element are the places to look, and water heater repair covers those checks.

Several baths also mean several shutoff valves under sinks and behind toilets. Each is a small part that can seep. Press a dry tissue against each one once a season and you will catch a slow drip before it reaches the cabinet floor.

Where is Orchard Park in Shafter?

Orchard Park is an LGI Homes community in ZIP 93263, listed as off South Central Valley Highway, with its sales center on Applegate Place. No public source gives a firm boundary, so name your street and the nearest cross street when you call.

How do I tell a yard leak from a house leak?

Turn off every fixture and appliance, read the water meter and read it again after twenty minutes. A moving dial means water is escaping. A soggy lawn or a sprinkler zone that never dries points outside, and a damp wall or cabinet points inside.

Do the larger plans need a bigger water heater?

That depends on what the builder installed and how many people use hot water at once. Check the capacity on the label. If hot water fades during busy mornings, a plumber can check the tank, the thermostat and the element before you replace anything.

Is a water softener worth it in a new house?

It depends on the water, and no verified hardness figure exists for Shafter. Test your tap first and read the city's annual water quality report. Equipment is sized after a test, not before.

Who should I call about a drip under a vanity?

Check the warranty first if the house is new. A drip at a supply line or shutoff valve is ordinary wear on any home, and a plumber can replace the part. Dry the area, mark the edge of the damp spot and note whether it grows. Whoever you hire, the Contractors State License Board lets anyone check a contractor's license on its Check A License page.
