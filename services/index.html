Drains, sewer lines and jetting compared

One slow sink points to drain cleaning, where a cable or hand auger clears the trap and branch line. When the tub, the toilet and the laundry all back up together, the blockage sits in the main, and sewer line cleaning with a larger machine is the better fit.

Grease, mineral scale and root hairs often return after a cable, because the cable only punches a hole. Hydro jetting scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water and suits lines that keep clogging every few months.

If the same line keeps failing, a sewer camera inspection shows whether the pipe is cracked or bellied. A sewer line repair only makes sense once the video proves the pipe itself is damaged.

Water heaters and leaking pipes

A tank that leaks from the bottom is usually past saving, so water heater replacement is the honest answer. No hot water with no leak is often a thermostat, element or pilot problem, and water heater repair costs far less than a new unit.

Planned upgrades go through water heater installation or tankless service, where you pick the unit before the old one fails. Summer heat in Shafter, with July highs near 99 F, is hard on closets and attics that hold a heater, so check the vent and the pan.

For hidden water, leak detection finds a running meter or a damp slab without opening walls. A fast burst pipe repair or slab leak repair follows once the source is clear.

Emergency visits and planned work

Emergencies are anything that floods, backs up with sewage, leaves a home without hot water or sends water through a ceiling. Shut off the nearest valve, stop running fixtures and call the 24/7 line. Same-day appointments are offered when available for work that can wait a few hours.
