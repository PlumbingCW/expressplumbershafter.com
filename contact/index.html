The fastest way to reach us

The only way to book is by phone. Call (855) 575-2890 and a dispatcher answers around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays. There are no web forms or booking pages to wait on.

Our address is 1160 Lerdo Hwy, Shafter, CA 93263. That is the dispatch base, not a walk-in showroom, so please call before you drive over. A general email address, contact@expressplumbershafter.com, is also posted for non-urgent questions.

What to say on the call

The dispatcher will ask a handful of questions. Having these answers ready helps.

  • Your address Include the town, the ZIP and a nearby cross street so the plumber finds the door quickly.
  • The symptom Say what you see and hear, such as water on the floor, a backed-up drain or no hot water.
  • What you already tried Mention a plunger, a closed valve or a reset button so the plumber does not repeat it.
  • How urgent it feels Active water or sewage is an emergency. A slow drip can often wait for a same-day appointment when available.

Not sure which service you need? Browse the plumbing services page, or just describe the problem and the dispatcher will match it.

While you wait for the plumber

Close the main water valve if water is escaping, and stop using fixtures that drain toward the problem. Keep children and pets away from wet floors, and stay clear of outlets near standing water.

If you smell gas, leave first and call from outside. SoCalGas customers can reach SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200, and 911 is the right call if anyone could be in danger.
