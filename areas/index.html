Home base on Lerdo Highway

Our dispatch line sits at 1160 Lerdo Hwy in Shafter, CA 93263. That ZIP is the only one the dispatch line itself counts as home turf, and the 24/7 line answers day and night. Shafter had 19,953 residents in the 2020 Census, and the city sits about 18 miles west-northwest of Bakersfield along State Route 43.

Lerdo Highway is the main east-west road through town and Highway 99 is the valley corridor to the east. Both matter when you give directions to a plumber, because a clear cross street saves time at the door. Mention the nearest named road or locality such as North Shafter, Myricks Corner, Smith Corner or Thomas Lane.

Wasco and McFarland, the two nearby towns

Wasco, ZIP 93280, is roughly 10 miles up State Route 43 from Shafter. Rose nurseries and farm properties there often have long irrigation runs and exposed hose bibs, so many calls start with a leaking outdoor line or a tired water heater. Our Wasco emergency plumber page explains what to do before the truck arrives.

McFarland, ZIP 93250, is roughly 15 miles from Shafter, with Highway 99 splitting the town into east and west sides. Backed-up sewer lines and hidden leaks are common reasons to call there, and the McFarland page covers how to help the plumber find your address.

Both distances are estimates, and actual drive time depends on traffic and the hour.

Which services travel with the truck

Most calls from all three towns fall into a few jobs. Drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning handle clogs, while water heater repair and replacement handle cold showers. Leak detection covers the quiet leaks that only show up on a water bill.

Shops and warehouses near the Minter Field industrial area or Paramount Logistics Park can use commercial plumbing after hours. The full list lives on our plumbing services page.

Calls from farther out

People in Delano, Lost Hills, Bakersfield, Oildale and Arvin sometimes call the line too. Calls from outside the main towns are taken by phone and handled case by case, and we make no promises about timing before the dispatcher knows your address and the problem.

If you are outside Shafter, Wasco or McFarland, tell the dispatcher your town and what is happening. A true emergency such as a burst pipe still gets the same advice about the main valve. Call (855) 575-2890 and the dispatcher will tell you plainly what can be done.
