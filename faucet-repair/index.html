A leaking or burst faucet valve with water running

Emergency faucet repair in Shafter, CA means a licensed plumber stops a faucet, shower valve or supply stop that is flowing, spraying or leaking and then repairs or replaces it. Our dispatcher answers 24/7. First close the small angle stop under the sink, turning it clockwise, and if that fails, shut the main valve where the water line enters the house.

Set a bucket under the leak and a towel across the cabinet floor. Skip the wrench on a corroded valve, since the stem can twist off. Call (855) 575-2890 and say which fixture is running and whether the main is off.

Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and a call after midnight or on a weekend gets the same dispatcher. The plumber who comes out brings cartridges, stems, supply lines and angle stops for common brands.

Cartridge, ball, ceramic disc or compression, what each one drips from

Four internal designs cover almost every faucet in a house. The one you own decides what fails.

Cartridge

A single handle that moves up and down and side to side. A drip means worn O-rings or a scored cartridge, and a new cartridge usually ends it.

Ball

A single handle on a rounded cap. Springs and neoprene seats wear, so the spout drips or the handle leaks at the base.

Ceramic disc

A quarter-turn single handle with a wide cylinder. Discs rarely wear, so a drip often comes from grit lodged between the discs or a failed seal.

Compression

Two handles that need real force to close. Rubber washers harden and the brass seat pits, which is why the faucet weeps.

Shower and tub valves, spouts and showerheads

A shower that drips after you turn it off usually has a worn cartridge or a tired diverter. A spout that leaks when the shower runs means the diverter in the tub spout is failing. A showerhead that drips often has a loose connection or a worn washer that a new thread tape wrap and washer fix.

A bathtub faucet repair that sends water through the wall is more urgent. If a stain grows on the other side of the wall or the ceiling below, close the main and call. A tub that will not drain also points to the trap, which a drain cleaning visit can open, though that sits outside the valve repair itself.

Angle stops, supply lines and the valve that broke

The angle stop under a sink, the small valve with a handle, is meant to isolate one fixture. They get turned rarely, so the stem corrodes and the packing leaks or snaps when someone finally tries. A burst or snapped stop sprays at full house pressure, which is why the main shutoff matters.

We replace the stop, swap the braided supply line, and test both for drips before we leave. Each connection gets a dry paper towel wrapped around it, since a slow weep shows as a damp spot long before it drips.

A stop that sits inside a finished cabinet with no access panel may need a small opening cut. If several fixtures in the house have stops that no longer close, plan for them all, and ask about plumbing installation when new shutoffs make sense alongside other work.

Hose bibs, laundry valves and the rare cold night

An outdoor hose bib, also called a spigot, drips from its packing nut or its washer. The washing machine valves behind the laundry box sit the same way. A bib that is left with a hose attached through cold weather can hold water that freezes.

Shafter rarely freezes hard. The record low is 14 F in December, and the January mean low is about 37 F, but exposed bibs can still crack on the coldest nights. Disconnect hoses in winter and slip a foam cover over each bib.

Watch for a hiss or a spray after the first cold snap. Our burst pipe crew covers burst pipe repair when a frozen line splits, and we also work in Wasco, roughly 10 miles up State Route 43.

What changes the price of a faucet visit

We quote a clear price before work starts. These factors move it.

  • Faucet type. A cartridge swap costs less than a full replacement.
  • Parts availability. Older or imported models may need ordering.
  • Valve condition. A seized stop or main adds work.
  • Wall access. A shower valve behind tile may need a hole in the wall.
  • Hour of the call. Overnight and weekend calls cost more than weekday visits.

Standard homeowner insurance usually covers sudden damage and not a worn washer or a slow drip, so check your policy. Call for the real number.

If parts are available and the body is sound, repair. A corroded body, a pitted seat or discontinued parts point to replacement.
