When the main line backs up in McFarland

A main sewer line that stops moving is the call we expect most from McFarland, CA, and the dispatch line answers it 24/7. Stop every faucet, flush and appliance at once, because wastewater that cannot leave the house will rise at the lowest opening, usually a shower drain or a floor drain.

Keep children and pets out of that room and do not pour chemical cleaner down a full drain. Call (855) 575-2890 and tell the dispatcher which fixtures gurgle or overflow, since that narrows where the stoppage sits. A plumber with a sewer machine is then assigned to the McFarland address.

Night and weekend calls reach the same dispatcher, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows. We lead with sewer line cleaning and leak detection here, and the two usually show up as a pair of symptoms.

Reading the symptom before the plumber does

What you see at home tells the plumber which tool to load on the truck.

Gurgling toilet after the washer runs

Air is being pushed back through the traps. That usually means a partial blockage on the main.

Shower drain bubbles when you flush

The shower sits lowest on the line, so it shows a main stoppage before any other fixture does.

Meter keeps turning with taps off

Water is escaping somewhere between the meter and the fixtures, which points to a supply leak.

Damp, warm spot on a floor

A hot water line may be leaking under the slab or in a wall. Call before the flooring lifts.

How a sewer line gets cleared

The plumber opens the cleanout, a capped pipe usually found near the foundation or at the property line, and feeds a drum machine with a cutter head into the line. Grease, roots and paper break up and flush downstream. If the stoppage is thick, a water jetter finishes the job. Sewer work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

A sewer camera follows, so the plumber can see whether the pipe has a crack, a sag or an offset joint. Without that step a cleared line may clog again in weeks. Read about the full process on the sewer line cleaning page.

If the camera shows a belly or a broken section, cleaning only buys time. Our sewer line repair page covers spot repair and replacement, and the plumber explains the trade-off before any digging starts.

Leaks that hide in the walls and slab

Not every McFarland call is a backup. Some are a water bill that doubled or a puddle that appears with no pipe in sight. A plumber starts with the meter test: shut every fixture, then watch whether the dial still moves.

From there, listening gear and pipe locators narrow the leak to a few feet. The leak detection visit finds the spot without tearing out a whole wall. If the leak is under the foundation, the next step is a slab leak repair with options to reroute or repair the line.

A worn toilet flapper can leak as much water as a cracked pipe and moves the meter dial the same way. The plumber checks the toilets first for that reason.

Highway 99 was built as an expressway in 1950 and later became a freeway, and it splits McFarland into east and west sides. For the dispatcher that matters at the very start of the call. Say which side of the highway you live on, and name the nearest cross street.

The parks make useful landmarks. Arturo J. Munoz Park, Ritchey Park and McFarland Park are all named on the city's listings, and a caller who says the house is two blocks from one of them saves the plumber a wrong turn.

From our Shafter office the drive is roughly 15 miles. We make no promise on arrival time, and the dispatcher tells you plainly when the plumber is on the way.

What to do before the plumber pulls up

A few quick jobs at the house make the visit smoother.

Locate the water shutoff

Check the meter box at the curb or a valve at the house wall. Practice turning it before you need it, since an old valve can stick.

Switch off nearby power

If water reached an outlet, a water heater or a washer, kill the breaker first and stay dry.

Free up the cleanout

Pull planters and boxes away from the cap by the foundation so the plumber can reach it.

Pause all drain use

No showers, laundry or dishwasher while the line is blocked, and no plunging a full toilet.

Photograph the damage

Snap the wet floor and any stained ceiling for your insurer, then ring the 24/7 line.

The town founded at Hunt's Siding

James Boyd McFarland, an educator, bought land near Hunt's Siding in 1909. The first post office opened in 1908, the town grew through the Depression and was incorporated on July 18, 1957. The city had a 2020 population of 14,161.

A town that is over a century old has laterals and service lines of different ages and routes. The plumber cannot assume anything about your pipe, so the camera view is how we learn it. The 2015 film McFarland, USA was based on a true story at McFarland High School,, though it tells you nothing about the pipes under your lot.

Your home's own history matters more. If you bought the house recently, ask the seller where the cleanout is and whether the sewer line has ever been inspected. For a planned check, see sewer camera inspection.

Water quality curiosity and what to read first

The City of McFarland posts water testing reports on its website, including quarterly reports on 1,2,3-trichloropropane, known as 1,2,3-TCP. Residents can read them before deciding on a filter. We state only that the reports exist and what they show is for you to read.

A filter or softener should answer a result you can read, not a guess. Test your tap, read the city report, then decide. The water softener and filtration page walks through that sequence and what a plumber installs.
