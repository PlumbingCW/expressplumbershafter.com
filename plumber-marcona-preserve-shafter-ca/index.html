Where Marcona Preserve runs along 7th Standard Road

Marcona Preserve is a Lennar community in Shafter, CA, ZIP 93263. The Wasco Tribune reported in July 2024 that it stretches from the Friant-Kern Canal to the Calloway Canal along Seventh Standard Road, with Jewetta Avenue North as the main street through it. It sits just down the road from Gossamer Grove.

The plan called for about 400 homes at the start and about 1,200 across three phases. Lennar's own page lists six house series with three to five bedrooms and says the community is actively selling, so the neighborhood is still filling in.

Newer streets can be missing from older maps. When you phone, give your street name, the cross street and Jewetta Avenue North if you are near it.

A first-year plumbing checklist for a new house

A home that is new to you needs a few minutes of plumbing homework in its first year. Find the main shutoff, find the water heater's label, and run every faucet and flush every toilet once to look for slow drains or drips. Photograph anything odd while the house is still under warranty.

Write down the model number and the serial number from the water heater label. A plumber, a manufacturer and a warranty claim all start with those two items.

If something is wrong, read the builder's warranty before you book outside work, because a third-party repair may affect coverage. For ordinary wear, such as a dripping faucet or a slow tub, a plumber can help directly. You can Call (661) 338-1180 to ask which way a problem leans.

Add-on units, extra fixtures and a bigger hot water load

The Wasco Tribune reported that some Marcona Preserve homes are energy-efficient models with add-on units. If your model has one, ask the builder which fixtures sit on their own shutoff and which share the main house line. That answer matters the first time you need to stop a leak without shutting off the whole house.

Homes with three to five bedrooms and several bathrooms put real demand on one water heater. If the hot water fades halfway through the second shower, the tank size, the thermostat setting or a failing element may be the cause. A plumber can tell which one before you replace anything.

Marcona Preserve plumbing questions

Where is Marcona Preserve?

Marcona Preserve runs between the Friant-Kern Canal and the Calloway Canal along Seventh Standard Road in Shafter, ZIP 93263, with Jewetta Avenue North as the main internal road. It is just down the road from Gossamer Grove.

Is Marcona Preserve finished?

Not according to Lennar, whose page lists the community as actively selling. The Wasco Tribune described three phases and about 1,200 homes, so construction and move-ins continue across the neighborhood.

What should I write down about a new water heater?

Write down the model number and serial number from the label on the tank, and photograph the label. A plumber or a manufacturer will ask for both, and having them ready shortens the call.

Do add-on units have separate plumbing?

That depends on the model. Ask the builder which fixtures have their own shutoff and whether the unit has its own water heater. A plumber can trace the lines in a house if the paperwork is unclear.

How can I tell a toilet leak from a supply leak?

Dry the floor and the base of the toilet, then put a few drops of food coloring in the tank. If color shows in the bowl without flushing, the flapper is leaking. If the floor is wet around the supply line or the base, the supply connection or the wax ring is the likelier cause.
