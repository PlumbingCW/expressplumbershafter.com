Where Gossamer Grove sits in Shafter

Gossamer Grove is a Lennar neighborhood in Shafter, CA, ZIP 93263. The Shafter Press placed it at the northwest corner of the city limits, near 7th Standard Road and Coffee Road, miles from the older core of town. Because it is far from downtown landmarks, give a cross street when you describe where you live.

The neighborhood was built in phases. In April 2019 the first phase of almost 500 homes was nearly sold out, a 173 home phase was selling and 323 more home sites were planned. A trade report from August 2026 says Lennar is advancing a final phase of more than 1,200 single-family lots under tentative city approval, so Gossamer Grove is still growing.

No public source gives a firm boundary. Lennar lists its sales address on Seco Canyon Way, so if you are unsure whether your street counts as Gossamer Grove, name the nearest cross street and the plan or series if you know it.

What newer construction changes about a plumbing call

Most Gossamer Grove homes are recent construction, so the first question is who is responsible. If the house or its water heater is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, read the warranty before you hire anyone, because outside repairs may affect coverage. Your closing paperwork shows who to contact for warranty items.

That does not make every problem a warranty item. A kitchen drain slowed by grease, a faucet cartridge that drips and a toilet flapper that no longer seals are ordinary wear on any home. When you Call (661) 338-1180, say which one you have and ask whether it sounds like a service call or a warranty question.

Summer heat, a hot-space water heater and a soggy lawn

Shafter summers are hard on equipment. The July mean daily high is about 99 degrees F and the record is 115, so wherever your water heater sits, whether a garage, a closet or a side yard, it works in hot air all summer. Keep the area around it clear, look at the base for rust or damp, and check the relief valve pipe for drips.

Winter is mild, but frost still happens. The January mean low is about 37 degrees F and the record low is 14, so an exposed hose bib can still freeze on the coldest night. If a strip of lawn or a planter stays wet, close the irrigation controller first, then read your water meter with every fixture off, because a moving dial points to a leak.

Gossamer Grove plumbing questions

Where is Gossamer Grove in Shafter?

The Shafter Press placed Gossamer Grove at the northwest corner of the city limits, near 7th Standard Road and Coffee Road. Lennar lists the community in ZIP 93263. No public source gives a firm boundary, so name your street and the nearest cross street when you call.

Should I call the builder or a plumber first?

If the house or an appliance is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, read the warranty first, because outside repairs may affect coverage. For ordinary wear such as a drip, a slow drain or a running toilet, a plumber can usually help directly. Ask when you call which way your problem leans.

Can I ask about timing before I book anything?

Yes. Timing depends on the schedule and on the problem, so ask when you call. Describe what is failing, give your street and the nearest cross street, and ask what the next step is.

How do I tell a yard leak from a house leak?

Turn off every fixture and appliance that uses water, then read the water meter and wait about twenty minutes. If the dial moved, water is escaping somewhere. A wet patch of lawn or a sprinkler zone that never dries points outside, and a damp wall or cabinet points inside.

Are there plumbing rules specific to Gossamer Grove?

This page does not list any. Ask the City of Shafter Building and Safety Division whether a job needs a permit, and check your closing paperwork for any neighborhood rules that apply to your lot.
